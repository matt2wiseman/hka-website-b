Medicare chronic condition plans

If you have a condition that has lasted, or is likely to last, six months or more, your GP may set up a GP chronic condition management plan and refer you to us for Medicare-rebated sessions.

Before your first visit

  1. Step 1

    See your GP

    Ask whether you're eligible for a GP chronic condition management plan that includes physiotherapy or exercise physiology. A longer GP appointment is usually needed.

  2. Step 2

    Get a referral

    Your GP writes a referral for physiotherapy, exercise physiology or both, with the number of sessions. It doesn't have to name us. Ask them to send it to [referrals@yourdomain.com.au].

  3. Step 3

    Book a Medicare GP plan appointment

    Choosing this appointment type lets us check your referral is in place before you arrive.

  4. Step 4

    Pay and claim on the day

    You pay our fee and we process your Medicare rebate on the spot [CONFIRM: Medicare Easyclaim], so you only carry the gap.

How your sessions work

Medicare rebates up to five allied health sessions per calendar year in total. Those five are shared across every allied health service on your plan, so three physio sessions and two podiatry sessions would use them all.

Each rebated session is at least 20 minutes. We send your GP a written report after your first and last sessions so your care stays joined up.

Five sessions is a solid start but often not a full rehab program. Many people continue privately, using their health fund for later sessions. We'll plan your sessions so the rebated ones count, for example a thorough assessment, a home program and spaced-out reviews.

Common questions

What counts as a chronic condition?
One that has been, or is likely to be, present for at least six months, such as osteoarthritis, persistent joint pain, diabetes or heart disease. Your GP decides whether you're eligible.
Is it bulk billed?
We don't bulk bill. You pay our fee and we process your Medicare rebate (currently about $62) on the spot, so you pay the difference: around $78 for an initial consultation and $38 for a follow-up.
I have an older GP Management Plan and Team Care Arrangement. Can I still use it?
Yes, for now. Plans in place before 1 July 2025 can be used until 30 June 2027. After that you'll need a GP chronic condition management plan.
Can I claim my health fund for the same session?
No. Each session can be claimed through Medicare or your health fund, not both. Once your rebated sessions are used, you can switch to your health fund.
Can I see both the physio and the exercise physiologist?
Yes, if your GP refers you to both. Your five sessions are shared between them, so we'll plan who you see and when.
Do the five sessions reset?
Yes, each calendar year (1 January). Your GP may need to review your plan and provide a new referral.
