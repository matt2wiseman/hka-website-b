Funding and referrals

Choose the way you're coming to us. Each guide covers what you need before your first visit, what it costs and how your sessions work.

  • Private or health fundPaying yourself or claiming through private health. No referral needed.
  • Medicare GP planRebated sessions through your GP's chronic condition management plan.
  • DVAVeteran Gold and White Card holders, with no gap to pay.
  • WorkCoverWorkplace injuries in Queensland, billed to the insurer.
  • Car accident (CTP)Motor vehicle accident injuries under Queensland's CTP scheme.
  • Sports injury insuranceClub and association personal injury cover.
