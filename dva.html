DVA clients

Before your first visit

  1. Step 1

    Get a referral

    Ask your GP for a referral to physiotherapy, exercise physiology or both. For your first treatment cycle, a referral from a treating specialist or hospital may also be accepted.

  2. Step 2

    Check your card

    A Gold Card covers clinically necessary treatment for all conditions. A White Card covers your accepted conditions only. You can check these in MyService.

  3. Step 3

    Book a DVA appointment

    Bring your card and referral, or ask your GP to send the referral to [referrals@yourdomain.com.au] before you come in.

  4. Step 4

    Nothing to pay

    We bill DVA directly. There is no gap fee.

How your sessions work

A DVA referral covers a treatment cycle of up to 12 sessions or 12 months, whichever comes first. Each cycle starts with an initial assessment.

At the end of the cycle we send your GP a report on your progress and our recommendations. If you need more treatment, your GP can provide a new referral to start another cycle.

TPI Gold Card holders are exempt from the treatment cycle for physiotherapy and exercise physiology. Call us and we'll confirm what you need.

Changes coming in July 2027

The Government has announced that the 12-session treatment cycle will be removed from July 2027, alongside a new annual limit on allied health spending for Veteran Card holders. We'll update this page once the details are confirmed.

Common questions

Will I have to pay anything?
No. When we accept your Veteran Card, we accept DVA's fee as full payment, so there is no gap.
I have a White Card. What can you treat?
Conditions DVA has accepted for you. We'll check your accepted conditions before your first session. If your hip, knee or ankle problem isn't covered, we can see you privately instead.
How many sessions can I have?
Up to 12 per treatment cycle, or 12 months, whichever comes first. A new referral from your GP starts a new cycle if you need more.
Can I see both a physio and an exercise physiologist?
Yes, with a referral for each. They work from the same plan, so your rehab stays joined up.

I'm a TPI Gold Card holder. Is anything different?
Yes. The treatment cycle doesn't apply to physiotherapy or exercise physiology for TPI Gold Card holders. Call us and we'll confirm what to bring.
