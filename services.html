What we offer

  • Physiotherapy assessment and treatment

    Diagnosis, hands-on treatment where it helps, and a clear exercise-based plan for injuries, pain and recovery after surgery.

    Good for: new injuries, pain you haven't had diagnosed, the first weeks after surgery

  • Exercise physiology

    Individual strength and conditioning programs designed around injuries, surgery and long-term health conditions, plus group classes for people aged 65 and over.

    Good for: later-stage rehab, chronic conditions, building strength safely

  • Before and after surgery

    Preparation and recovery for hip and knee replacement, ACL reconstruction, meniscus surgery, hip arthroscopy, Achilles repair and ankle fracture fixation. We follow your surgeon's protocol and keep them updated.

    Good for: anyone with surgery booked or recently done

  • Sports injuries and return to sport

    Rehab for sprains, strains, tendon pain and ligament injuries, with strength, hop and running tests to guide your return. Clearance letters for clubs when you're ready.

    Good for: club and social athletes, runners, junior sport

  • Osteoarthritis and joint health

    Education and structured strength programs for hip and knee osteoarthritis. [Name the GLA:D® program here only if your clinicians are certified — CONFIRM]

    Good for: stiff, sore joints; people told they'll "eventually need a replacement"

  • Strength and performance testing

    [Force plate and dynamometry — CONFIRM] testing to benchmark strength, power and side-to-side differences, for rehab milestones or performance goals. You get a plain-English report.

    Good for: return-to-sport decisions, pre-season screening, tracking progress

  • Exercise for chronic conditions

    Programs for diabetes, heart and lung conditions, osteoporosis and persistent pain, delivered by our exercise physiologists and often funded through a Medicare GP plan or DVA.

    Good for: managing a long-term condition with exercise

Beyond the lower limb, we also treat neck and back pain, shoulder and elbow injuries, and headaches that come from the neck.
