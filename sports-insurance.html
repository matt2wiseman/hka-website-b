Sports injury insurance

Before your first visit

  1. Step 1

    Tell your club straight away

    Ask your club secretary for the insurer's claim form. Many policies need you to notify them within about 30 days of the injury, so don't wait.

  2. Step 2

    Book in and bring the form

    We'll fill in the treating practitioner section for you. Your club completes its own declaration.

  3. Step 3

    Pay and claim your health fund

    Pay on the day and we'll claim your health fund rebate on the spot through HICAPS.

  4. Step 4

    Claim the rest from the insurer

    Send the completed form and your receipts to the insurer to claim back part of the remaining gap. Keep sending receipts as your rehab continues.

How your sessions work

Sports injury insurance usually pays a percentage of "non-Medicare" medical costs such as physiotherapy, after your health fund and minus an excess. Percentages, limits and excesses vary between sports and policies, so check your club's policy.

Insurers can't pay for anything Medicare has rebated, so a session claimed through a Medicare GP plan can't also go on your sports insurance.

Your rehab is built around getting you back on the field. We use strength, hop and sport-specific testing to guide your return, and can write a clearance letter for your club when you're ready.

Common questions

How do I find out who my club's insurer is?
Ask your club secretary or your sport's state association. They'll have the policy details and the claim form.
Do I need a doctor's referral?
Not to see us. Some policies ask for a doctor's statement on the claim form, so check the form before you start.
What if I don't have private health insurance?
You can usually still claim. Some policies apply a higher excess if you're not privately insured.
Is school or junior sport covered?
Registered junior players are often covered by their club's policy. Some schools have separate cover, so ask the school or club.
When can I get back to playing?
When your testing and training show you're ready, not just when a set number of weeks has passed. We'll agree on the milestones with you at the start, so you know what you're working towards.
