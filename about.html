About the clinic

Our approach

We help people across Brisbane trust their hips, knees and ankles again, with rehab that's tested, explained and progressed from the first session to the last.

A lot of rehab stalls in the gap between feeling better and being properly strong again. We set up the clinic to close that gap.

Because we're based inside 4PX Performance Gym in Coorparoo, your rehab doesn't stop at the treatment table. You'll test, train and progress in a fully equipped strength gym, with a physiotherapist and an exercise physiologist working from the same plan.

Every program starts with a thorough assessment and objective testing, so we know your starting point. From there we set clear milestones with you, re-test along the way and adjust, whether you're preparing for surgery, coming back from an ACL reconstruction or keeping a sore knee going so you can walk 18 holes.

What we stand for

  • Measure, don't guess

    We test strength, range and function at the start and along the way, so decisions are based on what your body is actually doing.

  • Explain everything

    You'll know what's going on, what the plan is, roughly how long it will take and what it will cost. No surprises.

  • Build it around exercise

    Hands-on treatment helps settle things down. A progressive exercise plan, coached properly, is what we build the rest of your rehab around.

  • One team around you

    Physios, exercise physiologists, your GP, surgeon, coach and case manager, all working from the same page.

  • Easy to access

    Private, Medicare, DVA, WorkCover, CTP or sports insurance. We handle the paperwork so you can focus on getting better.

From injury to full strength

Physiotherapy and exercise physiology overlap more than most clinics let them. We plan your rehab as one continuous path, and the right person leads each stage.

  1. Settle

    Calm things down, protect healing tissue and understand exactly what the problem is.

    Led by your physiotherapist

  2. Rebuild

    Restore movement, strength and control with a progressive plan, re-tested as you go.

    Physiotherapist and exercise physiologist together

  3. Perform

    Build the capacity for your sport, job or daily life, and the habits to keep it.

    Led by your exercise physiologist, with physio check-ins

Working with your other health professionals

Good rehab rarely happens in isolation. With your consent, we keep the people involved in your care up to date.

GPs

Reports after your first and final sessions, and whenever something changes.

Surgeons and specialists

Rehab that follows your surgeon's protocol, with updates at key milestones.

Coaches, clubs and schools

Clear return-to-training plans and clearance letters when you're ready.

Insurers and case managers

Timely treatment plans and progress reports for WorkCover, CTP and DVA.
