Before your first visit

  1. Step 1

    See a doctor

    Your doctor assesses the injury and gives you a work capacity certificate. You'll need it for your claim.

  2. Step 2

    Lodge your claim

    Lodge with WorkCover Queensland online or by phone, or with your employer's self-insurer if they have one. Keep your claim number handy.

  3. Step 3

    Book a WorkCover appointment

    Bring your claim number, work capacity certificate, employer details and your case manager's name if you have one.

  4. Step 4

    We bill the insurer

    Once your claim is accepted, we bill WorkCover or the self-insurer directly for approved treatment.

How your sessions work

After your claim is accepted, your first five physiotherapy consultations (including the first assessment) are pre-approved, as long as another allied health provider hasn't already treated the injury. If you need more, we send a management plan to your insurer for approval.

Exercise physiology works a little differently: the first consultation is pre-approved, then we request approval for the program that follows. After surgery, a new set of pre-approved physio sessions applies.

Throughout, we plan your rehab around the physical demands of your job and keep your doctor and case manager updated, so you can return to work safely and steadily.

Common questions

Do I need a GP referral?
Your claim needs a work capacity certificate from your doctor. Bring it, along with your claim number, to your first visit.
My claim hasn't been accepted yet. Can I still come in?
Yes. [CONFIRM your policy, e.g. "You pay on the day and we refund you once the claim is accepted. If it's not accepted, you can claim your health fund instead."]
How many sessions are covered?
Five physio consultations are pre-approved once your claim is accepted, if another allied health provider hasn't already treated the injury. We request approval for anything beyond that.
My employer is self-insured. Is that different?
The process is similar. We deal with your employer's claims team instead of WorkCover, so bring their contact details.
Can you help me get back to work?
Yes. We build your rehab around what your job asks of your body and share progress with your doctor and case manager. [CONFIRM if you offer worksite visits or functional capacity assessments.]
I'm a Commonwealth employee or my claim is interstate.
Comcare and interstate schemes have their own rules. Call us and we'll check what applies before you book.
