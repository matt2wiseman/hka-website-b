Physio or exercise physiologist?

Start with a physiotherapist if

You have a new injury, pain you haven't had diagnosed, or you've just had surgery. Physios assess and diagnose, use hands-on treatment where it helps, and set your rehab plan.

See an exercise physiologist if

You know what the problem is and need to build strength and fitness around it, or you're managing a chronic condition like osteoarthritis, diabetes or heart disease.

Still unsure? Book with a physio first and we'll bring in the right person as you go.
