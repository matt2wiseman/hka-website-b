Hips, knees and ankles are our focus

Most of our week is spent on the lower limb, so we see these problems often and know how they tend to respond. We look after the rest of the body too.

Hip

  • Hip and groin pain
  • Hip osteoarthritis
  • Gluteal tendon pain
  • Hamstring injuries
  • Rehab after hip arthroscopy or replacement

Knee

  • ACL injury and reconstruction rehab
  • Meniscus and ligament injuries
  • Kneecap (patellofemoral) pain
  • Patellar tendon pain
  • Knee osteoarthritis and replacement rehab

Ankle and foot

  • Ankle sprains and instability
  • Achilles tendon pain and rupture
  • Plantar heel pain
  • Fracture rehab
  • Shin pain and bone stress injuries

Your first few weeks

  1. Step 1

    Book online or call

    Choose your pathway, a time and a practitioner. A short intake form means your session isn't spent on paperwork.

  2. Step 2

    A thorough assessment

    45–60 minutes, including strength and movement testing. You leave with a clear explanation, a plan and your first exercises.

  3. Step 3

    Rehab you can measure

    We re-test regularly so you can see what's changing. Your physio and exercise physiologist work from the same plan.

  4. Step 4

    Back to what you love

    Return to sport, work or everyday life is guided by your results and goals, not just the calendar.
